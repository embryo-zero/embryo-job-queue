# Embryo Job Queue

Embryo Job Queue is Embryo-Zero's fork of [slurm](https://github.com/SchedMD/slurm) (14d4961, commit 14d4961). It does the batch scheduler our cluster already uses.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING, LICENSE.OpenSSL) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
